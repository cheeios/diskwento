Overview

Introducing Diskwento

Diskwento is a free mobile application that helps Persons with Disability (PWD) and Senior Citizens in the Philippines verify whether their statutory 20% discount and VAT exemption were correctly applied on their receipts.

Version 1.0.0 marks the first public beta release, focused on the two core flows: checking if you were charged correctly, and filing a formal complaint if you were not.

Features

What's Included

♿

Discount Checker

Enter your items and the amount charged on your receipt. Diskwento calculates what you should have paid — including the 20% discount and VAT exemption — and tells you instantly if you were overcharged.

  • Supports both PWD and Senior Citizen discount types
  • Handles VAT-inclusive and VAT-exclusive prices per item
  • Shows exact breakdown: base price, VAT removed, discount applied, correct total
  • Highlights overcharge amount in red if applicable
🚩

Report Filing

If overcharged, file a formal complaint or concern directly from the app to the relevant government agencies with a single tap. All reports are pre-filled with your transaction details.

  • Recipients: DTI, NCDA, PDAO (local), OSCA (local)
  • Choose complaint type: formal complaint or inquiry/concern
  • Attach GPS-tagged receipt photos as evidence
  • Specify the establishment name and address
  • Select your city for routing to local PDAO/OSCA
  • Report logged automatically to Diskwento records

Legal Basis

Statutory Foundation

All discount calculations in Diskwento are based on the following Philippine laws:

RA 9994

Expanded Senior Citizens Act of 2010 — Grants senior citizens (60 years old and above) a 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicine, food, services, and other specified goods.

RA 7277

Magna Carta for Persons with Disability — As amended by RA 9442 and RA 10754, grants PWD cardholders a 20% discount and VAT exemption on medicine, medical services, and other covered establishments.

Known Limitations

Beta Limitations

v1.0.0 is a Beta release. The following features are not yet available and are planned for the next version:

  • Transaction history — no saved records yet
  • Document storage — prescriptions and IDs cannot be saved in-app
  • VAT Calculator — standalone tax breakdown tool not yet available
  • Android support — iOS only in this release

Disclaimer

Important Note

Diskwento is a verification and reporting aid only. It does not constitute legal advice. Discount rates and covered establishments may be subject to change based on government directives. Always verify current rules with the relevant agencies (DTI, NCDA, DOH).

Report filing through the app uses your device's mail application. Diskwento does not guarantee response times from government agencies.
